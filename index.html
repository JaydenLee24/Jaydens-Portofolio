About Me

I’m a digital illustrator focusing on stylized characters, cinematic art direction, and emotionally driven compositions. I combine soft lighting, noir ambiance, and subtle color contrast to create a unique visual identity. Now, I dive deeper For Ciinematic Arts. I was once to be known by the name "Yuuta" and "Jyn".

Tools I use: Ibis Paint, Clip Studio Paint, and Blender for lighting reference.
